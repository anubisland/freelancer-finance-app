# Getting Started with Freelancer Finance

This guide walks you through setting up your account and performing core tasks in the Freelancer Finance App.

## 1. Create Your Account

1. Download the app from the [iOS App Store](#) or [Google Play Store](#), or visit the [web app](#).
2. Open the app and tap **Sign Up**.
3. Sign up with email and password, or use Google Sign-In.
4. Verify your email address if prompted.
5. Complete your profile: set your business name, default currency, and tax rate.

## 2. Add Your First Client

1. From the main dashboard, navigate to the **Clients** tab.
2. Tap **Add Client** (or the **+** button).
3. Fill in the client's name, email, phone, and address.
4. Tap **Save**. The client now appears in your client list.

## 3. Create an Invoice

1. Navigate to the **Invoices** tab and tap **Create Invoice**.
2. Select a client from your client list.
3. Add line items — describe the work, set the quantity and rate.
4. Review the subtotal, tax, and total.
5. Set the due date.
6. Tap **Save** to create a draft, or **Send** to email it to the client.

## 4. Log an Expense

1. Navigate to the **Expenses** tab and tap **Log Expense**.
2. Enter the amount, select a category, and add a description.
3. Optionally attach a receipt by tapping the camera icon.
4. Toggle **Tax Deductible** if the expense qualifies.
5. Tap **Save**.

## 5. View Reports

1. Navigate to the **Reports** tab.
2. Select a report type: Income, Expense, Profit & Loss, or Tax Summary.
3. Choose a date range.
4. Tap **Generate** to view the report.

## Device Sync

Your data syncs automatically across all devices where you're signed in. Changes made on your phone appear instantly on the web app and vice versa. Firestore's offline persistence lets you work without a connection — changes sync when you're back online.
